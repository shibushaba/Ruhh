These files are subsets of SIL Open Font License 1.1 families.
The license text is OFL.txt in this folder. The fonts are bundled so the
app does not download them at runtime.

- Mochiy Pop One (titles and numbers)
- Zen Maru Gothic (body and labels)
- Kiwi Maru (companion lines)
- Noto Sans Malayalam (Malayalam, and the rupee sign U+20B9)
- Noto Naskh Arabic (Arabic)

Subsets keep Latin, punctuation, currency (including ₹), Malayalam, and Arabic.
